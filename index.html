About SKILL

Seminar Keilmiahan dan Hasil Penelitian

Seminar Keilmiahan dan Hasil Penelitian (SKILL) is an exhibition, seminar, and award event involving students and the public, aimed at educating the importance of research while presenting the works of the Kelompok Peneliti Muda Universitas Negeri Jakarta. The event is planned as an in-person forum for interaction, idea exchange, and appreciation of scholarly work.
